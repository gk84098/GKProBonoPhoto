About the project

I'm a photography enthusiast based in Park City — not a professional, but someone who takes the craft seriously and wanted to put it to use for causes that matter.

This project offers free photography to local nonprofits: event coverage, staff and volunteer portraits, and images for websites, social media, and grant applications.

If your organization works in or around Park City, I'd love to help.
